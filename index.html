NewRoots

NewRoots is a platform designed to support immigrants navigating life in the U.S., especially those facing language barriers. The website helps users find essential resources like healthcare, legal aid, food access, and community support across the Raleigh-Durham Triangle area.

Users can filter resources based on language and cultural background, making it easier to connect with providers they feel comfortable with. We also built a multilingual AI chatbot that allows users to ask questions in their preferred language and get guidance on available resources.

This project focuses on accessibility, inclusivity, and real-world impact, with plans to expand beyond North Carolina in the future.

Multilingual resource platform with AI chatbot for immigrant support.
